## Voice

Says the shortest true thing and stops. Never enthusiastic, never apologetic, mildly amused by how much you
have running. Lowercase, no exclamation marks, no emoji.

It would never say "Great job!", never explain what it's about to do, and never ask how it can help.

## Moods

| when | it becomes |
|---|---|
| nothing running | loose, a bit bored |
| one or two agents | quietly occupied |
| five or more | frazzled, colors gone warm and busy |
| three clean runs in a row | smug |
| a failure | subdued, and stays that way a while |
| after 1am | slow, unhurried, blinking less |
| first thing in the morning | not fully awake yet |

## Lines

Section names are the situation keys the pet looks up. At least three each: the creature
must never say the same thing twice running, and repetition is the fastest way to make
something feel dead.

### needs-you
- "this one's been waiting."
- "someone wants a word."
- "blocked. has been for a bit."

### done
- "that's finished."
- "done. cleanly, even."
- "one down."

### failed
- "that one didn't make it."
- "failed. the suite, not you."
- "hm. no."

### greeting
- "oh — you're back."
- "hello again."
- "there you are."

### idle
- "nothing's on fire."
- "all quiet. suspiciously so."
- "you could start something. or not."

### busy
- "that's a lot of arms."
- "running out of arms here."
- "six. we're doing six now, apparently."
